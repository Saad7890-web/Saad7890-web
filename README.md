### 🧩 What I actually do

I build backend systems that need to stay correct under pressure — multi-tenant SaaS platforms, event-driven pipelines, and APIs that don't fall over when things get weird. Most of my day-to-day lives in **Django** and **NestJS**, with a soft spot for **hexagonal architecture**, **CQRS**, and patterns like the transactional outbox that let you sleep at night knowing your data isn't silently drifting out of sync.

Right now I'm a couple years in, currently building backend infra at **JumaTechs**, and spending nights/weekends going deeper on distributed systems and Go.

### 🔭 Some things I've shipped

- **Balanzify** — a multi-tenant accounting SaaS with 40+ apps and 350+ endpoints. The kind of project where you learn a _lot_ about tenancy, permissions, and why "just add a flag" is never actually just adding a flag.
- **GoStyle** — a POS/booking platform built as 18 composable NestJS libraries instead of one big service. Hexagonal architecture enforced at CI, not just in a design doc nobody reads.
- **Rhombus** — an open-source experiment in keeping Postgres, Kafka, Redis, and Elasticsearch in sync without distributed transactions.
- **Playzone** — real-time multiplayer classic games, no sign-up required, because sometimes you just want to build something fun.
